Welcome to CineMatch
Your Personalized Movie Recommendation App

CineMatch is a Flask-based application designed to provide personalized movie recommendations based on user preferences. Utilizing data from the TMDb API and trailers via the YouTube API, our platform ensures you get the best movie suggestions tailored just for you.

Users can register, log in, manage their watchlist, and receive customized movie recommendations. CineMatch also allows users to write and share reviews, creating a community-driven movie discovery experience.

With a vast collection spanning multiple genres and languages, CineMatch is your go-to platform for discovering amazing films suited to your tastes.
